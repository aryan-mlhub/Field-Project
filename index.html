Water Science Essentials

TDS

What is TDS?

Total Dissolved Solids refers to inorganic mineral salts (calcium, magnesium, sodium) in water.

Ideal: 50-300 ppm | Max (BIS): 500 ppm
pH

What is pH?

pH measures how acidic or alkaline water is on a 0-14 scale. 7.0 is neutral.

Safe Drinking Range: 6.5 to 8.5
BIS

BIS 10500 Standards

Mandatory Indian national benchmarks for potable tap and groundwater quality safety.
